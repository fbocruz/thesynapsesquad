Privacy Policy – The Synapse Squad

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

The Synapse Squad is a YouTube channel with animated videos about emotions and brain chemistry. "Synapse Squad Esteira" is an internal tool, used only by the channel owner to manage the channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it or use it.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Synapse Squad Esteira uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

3. What data the app accesses

With the permission granted by the channel owner through Google sign-in, the app accesses:

  • basic channel information: the channel name and its identifier;
  • metadata of the channel's own videos: titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and status (for example, private or public).

This access uses two YouTube permissions (scopes): youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. The app does not access data from other channels or from viewers.

4. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos to the channel and to update the title, description, translations, captions and thumbnails of the channel's own videos.

5. Storage and sharing

  • The authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any server of ours. The app talks only to the YouTube API.
  • The app keeps a local log with only the date, the type of operation and the video ID of each request.

6. Cookies

This website and the app do not use cookies or trackers.

7. Retention and deletion

  • The data stored locally by the app (the authorization token) is deleted when access is revoked.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time on Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • You can ask for the deletion of any data related to you by writing to thesynapsesquadchannel@gmail.com. We will reply within 7 days.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about this policy: thesynapsesquadchannel@gmail.com.
